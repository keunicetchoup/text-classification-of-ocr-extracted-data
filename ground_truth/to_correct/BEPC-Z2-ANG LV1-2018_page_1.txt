A UNIVERSAL LANGUAGE ?

People have dreamed of a universal language since the 17" century. Many artificial
languages like Esperanto have been invented. But they haven't had much success, mainly
because they are not based on any common culture or experience.

Some people have suggested that English should be the Universal Language. It is
spoken regularly by several hundred million people in the whole world, and it is the
official language of many countries. It is taught in most European and Asian schools. Its
grammar is quite simple and its vocabulary cosmopolitan. If English is chosen as the
Universal Language, what kind of English will be spoken? American English or British
English? American English has become very different from British English, not just in
the way words are pronounced and printed, but in the grammar and vocabulary that are used
too.

Around the world, English speakers are creating their own kinds of English. For
example, an Anglo-Indian phrase is “as honest as an elephant”, and an Anglo-African
expression is “to eat each other’s ears,” meaning “to listen carefully.”

One thing is certain to most people today: learning English is a “must”.

Adapted from Channel 3, P 21.
