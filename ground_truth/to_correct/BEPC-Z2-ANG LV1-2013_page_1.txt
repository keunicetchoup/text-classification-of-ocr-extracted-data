KAMAU WANTS TO GET MARRIED

The group of women continued to talk quietly amongst themselves. The group of
elders seemed excited as they drank the palm-wine. Muthoni, Wambu’s mother looked at
Kamau. His eyes were on one man who was staring greedily at the meat and talking
excitedly at the same time. Muthoni could almost guess what was going on in his mind.

5 The meat these men were eating and the wine they were drinking must have cost Kamau
a lot of money.

She prayed deep in her heart that the elders changed their minds about the dowry.
Today, they had decided he would have to pay 250,000FCFA on the following Sunday.
Along with this, he would have to bring two goats to be slaughtered and some beer and

10 food. It is only after paying all the dowry that Wangi, Muthoni’s husband and the elders
would consent to the marriage. Kamau had pleaded with them, saying that he could not
afford all that much. He was now waiting for their final decision. “How greedy these
men are !’? Muthoni thought. ‘‘What do they think the young man is, a factory manager?
Don’t they know he is only a junior clerk in the factory? Couldn’t they ...?” Suddenly her

15 thoughts were interrupted by her husband’s voice.

“Young man, we still maintain our first proposal. We cannot alter it. We are in fact

“being very kind. Wambui is an educated girl and she ought to bring a good price”'. He
glanced at Muthoni and continued: “Her mother can tell you how hard-working she is.
She will make a perfect wife. What do you say, young man?”

20 “T still want to marry your daughter” he replied, “but I’m sorry I can’t afford the
dowry. I do not have ready money. I beg you to consent to the marriage and I will pay
what I can and I promise to pay the rest gradually”.

Adapted from A girl For Sale and Other Stories by Daniel WAMBEU.
